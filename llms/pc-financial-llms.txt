# PC Financial (President's Choice Financial)

> PC Financial is the digital-first, branchless financial-services brand of Canadian grocery giant Loblaw Companies Limited. Its personal banking and Mastercard credit-card products are issued by President's Choice Bank, a federally chartered Schedule I bank and CDIC member. The brand centers on the no-fee PC Money Account, PC Financial and PC World Elite Mastercard credit cards, and deep integration with the PC Optimum loyalty program. PC Financial publishes NO public first-party developer portal or API; under Canada's not-yet-operational Consumer-Driven Banking framework, consumer account access today is aggregator/screen-scraping-based via providers such as Plaid and Finicity (Mastercard). It supports Interac e-Transfer on the PC Money Account as shared payments-rail participation.

## Status
- No public API, developer portal, OpenAPI, GraphQL, or MCP server exists (verified 2026-07-23).
- Consumer banking (chequing, savings, mortgages) was transferred to CIBC's Simplii Financial in 2017.
- In December 2025 Equitable Bank (EQB) agreed to acquire PC Financial from Loblaw for roughly $800 million.
- Canada's Consumer-Driven Banking (open banking) framework is legislated but not yet operational.

## Data access (aggregator-based)
- No first-party consumer-data API. Third-party account aggregation is performed by Plaid and Finicity (Mastercard) via credential-based/screen-scraping connections.
- Interac e-Transfer is supported on the PC Money Account as shared payments-rail participation, not as a public API surface.
